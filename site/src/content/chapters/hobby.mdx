On 14 November 2022 the *Southern Metropolis Daily* (南方都市报) ran a story headed "帆板、露营之后，年轻人又开始戳戳秀了": after paddleboarding and camping, young people turn to poke-poke.[@ner-cn-pokepoke] 戳戳秀, also written 戳戳绣, "poke-poke", was the name under which tufting with a hand gun became a craze in mainland China. According to the 2022 press, tufting studios opened first in Hangzhou, then in Guangzhou, where one shop opened in September 2021, and by early 2022 in Hefei and Jinan. The papers credited the trend to overseas social media, Instagram, TikTok and Xiaohongshu, and to the artist Tim Eads, in 2018.[@ner-cn-pokepoke] Eads is the author of this book, and the attribution is the press's, not a measured influence.[@co-tuft-the-world]

## Home tufting before the gun

Making pile rugs at home with a hand tool was not new. A "Jewel" rug-hooking tool, patented in the United States in 1886 and described by Auckland Museum as a semi-automated tool for punching pile through backing, was picked up in Auckland in 1930.[@wc-83070426] The "Susan Burr" was a boxed hand rug machine with printed instructions, of a type dated to the 1920s: two wooden handles, a needle in one and a gauge in the other, worked alternately.[@ttw-susan-burr]

<Figure id="ttw-ronco-speed-tufting-kit" image={1} caption="Contents of the Ronco kit: the moulded-plastic tufting tool, fabric shredder, yarn, rug needle, latex sample, printed burlap backing and pattern sheet." />

In 1975 Ronco sold a "Speed-Tufting Kit" on television for $9.95. It held a moulded-plastic tool worked by a needle handle and a looper handle in turn, a printed burlap backing, a fabric shredder for cutting old clothes, sheets or blankets into strips to tuft with, and an order sheet for patterns by mail.[@ttw-ronco-speed-tufting-kit] The patent probably behind it, filed by John S. Doyel in October 1975, was titled "Rug-tufting device suitable for home use by hobyists".[@pat-us3968757a; @ttw-ronco-speed-tufting-kit]

These were manual tools. The powered gun has a separate history, told in chapter 9: according to the Hong Kong Heritage Project, Tai Ping fitted the tufting gun with a motor and reduced its weight in 1973, though other sources give other dates.[@ner-hk-taiping-1973] The records gathered for this book find the gun, before 2018, in workshops and mills rather than homes. Kramis in Switzerland makes bespoke rugs with single-needle tufting guns, and Shaw's designers say hand guns still mend broadloom at final inspection.[@co-kramis; @finding-shaw-mending-gun]

## The gun goes home

Tuft the World was founded in West Philadelphia in 2018 by the artists Tiernan Alexander and Tim Eads. It sells tufting tools, materials and instruction and runs workshops, and it says its aim is to make tufting accessible, with a focus on equity and sustainability.[@co-tuft-the-world] In March 2024 it gave a lecture and rug-tufting workshop at The Fabric Workshop and Museum.[@co-tuft-the-world] The next month Chronicle Books published the founders' illustrated manual for tufting with a hand gun, *Tuft the World: An Illustrated Manual to Tufting Gorgeous Rugs, Decor, and More*.[@book-tuft-the-world-2024]

Social media carried studios as well as hobbyists. A tufting video that Kramis posted on Instagram in 2022 brought the Swiss studio, founded in 1987, international attention (chapter 11).[@co-kramis] How many people took up the hobby is not yet known: no sales or user figures have been found.

## Who makes the guns

The tools come from older tufting centres. Sheru Bros Engineering Works of Meerut, established in 1965 according to its own listing (another directory says fourteen years), advertises electric hand tufting guns on IndiaMART, one at about ₹9,000, and says it exports them to Egypt and South Africa. Every one of those details is the seller's own.[@ner-in-sheru] Manual tools with the same two-part alternating action as the Ronco kit are still sold new in Mirzapur: Naksh Enterprises offers one for cut and loop pile up to 45 mm, at around ₹1,500.[@ttw-mirzapur-manual] Chapter 10 follows the Indian industry they serve.

## Back on the machine

<Figure id="pat-cn111705448a" size="margin" caption="Yangxin Ruixin Group, CN 111705448 A, a pneumatic tufting gun for a carpet robot, published 25 September 2020." />

The same years put the gun back on machines. In June 2020 the Yangxin Ruixin Group, a Shandong carpet company, filed for a "pneumatic multifunctional carpet tufting gun for an intelligent gun-tufted carpet robot", published that September.[@pat-cn111705448a] It is one of a cluster of Chinese filings for automated gun-tufting: a yarn-feeding and tensioning device for a gun-tufting machine, filed in January 2019 according to a search summary, and a "fully automatic carpet gun-tufting machine head", published in November 2020.[@pat-cn111441143a; @pat-cn111876919a]

In 2023 Tuft the World showed editioned rugs made on a large robotic tufting machine at Paradigm Gallery in Philadelphia. Eads said the company had been "innovating ways to push the boundaries of what is possible with tufting and textiles."[@event-ttw-editions-2023] Chapter 12 follows the machines.

This chapter argues that after 2018 the hand tufting gun, until then found in workshops and mills, became a home pastime spread by social media and small supply firms, and reached mainland China as poke-poke by 2021, while in the same years the gun was being mounted on machines again.
