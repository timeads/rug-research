# A History of Tufting (public site)

Scope: the public site in `site/` (cover, chapters, timeline, bibliography, record pages), published at /rug-research/book/. Visitor mode: Read.

Audience and job: scholars first (fellowship reviewers, curators, textile historians), makers second; they read chapter by chapter and follow any note to its source. Must not feel like a template or AI site, or like a Tuft the World brand site. Related to the research tool's world (DESIGN.md) without copying it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The history is a dye house's shade card: every chapter is one wound shade on a single card, picked and read. It refuses the cream-paper editorial long-read (serif display on off-white, italic deks, small tracked labels over headings).

OWN-WORLD: Cool card-stock grey ground, never cream; a charcoal bookcloth board for masthead and foot. Thirteen chapter shades from the research tool's bookcloth palette, each shown as a wound-yarn band of exact parallel threads with its shade number. Archivo (condensed widths) for the frame and headings, Literata for reading, tabular numerals for dates and shade numbers.

STORY: The reader sees the whole history as one card of thirteen shades, picks a chapter, reads it in a calm column with sources beside the text, and reaches any record in one step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cover: board masthead with title and contents; the title and one-line scope; the shade card of thirteen bands below, the ready chapter at full strength, the rest marked in preparation. Chapter: masthead, the chapter's wound band with shade number and years, title, dek, byline, then the opening photograph and the reading column with margin notes.

FORM: candidate 3 of 7 (the dye house's yarn shade card). seed:79f42d0668c4b726

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
