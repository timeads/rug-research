Research notes

Classified advertisement (from the search snippet): "Bedspreads Wanted. We want knotted and tufted bedspreads. Do not send any spreads without first consulting ..." Repeated in issues of Nov. 6, Dec. 4, 11, 18, 1919, and Jan. 1 and 8, 1920. Who placed the ad is not yet read from the page. Why it matters: Dalton bedspreads: shows a buyer for hand-made "tufted bedspreads" in the Appalachian craft trade in 1919, before Dalton's 1920s boom. Tagged possible until the full ad is read. Found: Chronicling America, search "tufted bedspreads", 2026-10-28.
