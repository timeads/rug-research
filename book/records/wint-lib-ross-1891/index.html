Research notes

Mail-order catalog sold with Ebenezer Ross's rug machine; mostly patterns carried over from earlier catalogs, plus his own originals "Mastiff Dog" and "Lion and Palm" (later copied by Montgomery Ward and Sears). Winterthur Library copy, digitized on the Internet Archive.
