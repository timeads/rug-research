Research notes

Checked: the Victoria County History (Wiltshire vol. 6, 'Wilton: Industry since c.1500') says that in 1699 the town sought a charter to restrict entry into the weaving trade to members of the fellowship, because outside competition had overwhelmed the old by-laws; at least 37 Wilton weavers and 25 from the surrounding area joined that year. Wilton Carpets' timeline says William III granted the clothiers and weavers a charter 'to reduce the impact of foreign competition'; another source says it limited trade within four miles to those who had served a seven-year apprenticeship. Correction: the charter concerned the woollen-cloth trade generally, not carpet weaving, which the VCH dates from 1741. The claims that a carpet factory was built at Wilton in 1655 (Wilton Carpets; Britannica's 'Royal Carpet Factory established 1655') are not supported by the VCH.
