Research notes

This is the founding patent of the Hofmann hand-tufting gun lineage. Hofmann Handtuft-Technik's history page says the firm began in 1973 as Scholz Handtuft-Technik KG in Allersberg, with Hartmut ("Harmut") Scholz and Heinz Hofmann as managing directors. This patent was filed by Scholz from Allersberg in the same year. Its US counterpart is US3968758A, "Arrangement for the tufting of carpets" (13 Jul 1976). Wilcom's later patents (EP1674605, US8225727) cite "the Scholz (US 3,968,758)" gun as an earlier hand-held tufting gun whose cutter could be engaged and disengaged by hand. Later German filings citing it include DE2621360A1 and DE3410783A1.
