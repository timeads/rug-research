Research notes

Photograph. Catalogue description: "Julia Mitchell using a hand-held tufting gun to stitch in a floral pattern on a bedspread at Cabin Crafts. The bedspread is held in place within a circular frame that grants Mitchell easier access to all areas of the bedspread. A second tufting operator sits at a frame next to Mitchell, and an empty frame is positioned next to the second woman. A supervisor stands behind the second operator viewing her progress." Date: c.1940. Why it matters: Hand tools and the gun, open questions "the earliest dated motorised or air tufting gun in production use" and "a dated photograph of a hand gun in use in Dalton": a hand-held tufting gun in factory use c. 1940, matching the Vanishing Georgia "electric needlepunch gun" photograph of Cabin Crafts, c. 1941 (wtf225). Related: Cabin Crafts workers "using air-driven hand tufters" on round frames (Deaton Collection 2010.003.0095c, undated).
