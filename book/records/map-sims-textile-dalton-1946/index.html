Research notes

A map of Dalton issued by Sims Textile, reproduced or cited by Deaton "courtesy of Dalton-Whitfield County Historical Society". Likely shows the bedspread businesses at their peak, and ties in with the Sims sheets at the Bandy Heritage Center (own-bandy-sims-chenille-center). Cited in Deaton, Bedspreads to Broadloom (1993), bibliography pp. 209–217 (transcribed in research/deaton-sources.md).
