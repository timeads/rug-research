Research notes

What it shows: an early wearing blanket in tapestry weave with wide horizontal bands. Gift of Mary Cabot Wheelwright, 1942. Other dated examples: Maxwell Museum (UNM) 63.34.114, a First Phase chief blanket of c. 1850–1860. Penn Museum NA3477, 'First Phase Chief Blanket, Ute type', 1800–1850. The Bern Historical Museum 'Schoch' blanket (1890.410.0027), c. 1830, once catalogued as a 'Sioux trade cloth'.
