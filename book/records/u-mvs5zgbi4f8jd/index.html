Research notes

MFA record: "Bed Rugg / Bed Cover", Spanish, before 1683; cotton (Z single); "plain weave with supplementary weft pile (10 low spun singles used to make pile yarn)"; 284.5 x 212.6 cm. Description: "The ground and the design are both of white cotton. The design is woven with a pile like velvet. ... In the middle is a lozenge bearing a crowned animal, two birds and a flower. Made in three breadths and sewed together. Lined with heavy white cotton." Gift of Robert C. Winthrop (the "before 1683" date presumably from Winthrop family provenance; not stated). Why it matters: Before the machine: a white cotton woven-pile bed covering called a "bed rugg", dated before 1683, i.e. a European antecedent of white tufted/candlewick spreads and of the term "bed rugg". Tagged possible (woven, not needle-made). Found: MFA Boston collections search, "bed rugg", 2026-10-28.
