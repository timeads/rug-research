Research notes

Downgraded to suggestive: every detail is the seller's own listing on IndiaMART; the price and the motor specs come from different listings. Electric hand tufting guns, ~₹9,000, 300 W, 1425 rpm, exported to Egypt and South Africa. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): partly confirmed. Every detail is self-reported on IndiaMART (a commercial marketplace), so 'solid' is too strong. Est. 1965 (partnership firm, Meerut) appears, but ExportersIndia says '14 years'. ₹9,000 is the 'Blue Hand Tufting' gun. 1425 rpm is on that listing, while 300 W is on a different gun listing, so the two specs may not belong to one product. 'Exports to Egypt and South Africa' is the seller's own claim. Source: Sheru Bros Engineering Works, Meerut – Tufting Gun Machine (IndiaMART seller listing).
