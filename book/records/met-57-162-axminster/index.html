Research notes

Hand-knotted wool carpet, 23 × 16 ft (701 × 487.7 cm), bought with funds from various donors in 1957; not on view. Tentative attribution ('probably'). The best Met example of the knotted 'Axminster' that loom-woven Wilton and Brussels competed with on price. Sources: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/202045 Research note (Oct 2026): from search results; pages not opened. See also met-928622: the Met's second Whitty carpet, ca. 1765, acquired 2025 (the picture shown here is of that carpet).
