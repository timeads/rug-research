Research notes

Hand-knotted wool carpet ("Wool; knotted pile"), attributed to Thomas Whitty of the Axminster carpet manufactory, ca. 1765 (the Met's date range 1760–1770). Acquired by the Met in 2025: "Purchase, F.J. Hakimian Gift, in memory of Joseph Hakimian, and Christian Keesee and Anonymous Gifts, in memory of Irene Roosevelt Aitken, 2025". Gallery 511. Public domain image. Why it matters: a dated Whitty attribution from his first decade at Axminster (his first carpet was finished in 1755), a generation earlier than the Met's other Whitty carpet (57.162, ca. 1780–90). Source: Met Collection API, object 928622 (record dated 2026-07-16), saved in research/fetched.
