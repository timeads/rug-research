Research notes

Founded in 1989 as Roger Oates Design Associates, a studio that employed handloom weavers and hand-tufters; moved to the Long Barn on the Eastnor Castle estate in 1993–94, London showroom 1999, Ledbury showroom 2022. Best known for flatweave runners; its hand-tufted rugs (British wool) are made in a company workshop in Devon. Why it matters: one of few UK firms documented as hand-tufting domestically; most British carpet history is woven (Axminster, Wilton). Dates: 1989 (Wikipedia); 1993 vs 1994 for the Eastnor move. The Devon workshop's start date not found.
