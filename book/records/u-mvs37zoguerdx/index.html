Research notes

Photograph with caption. Hagley summary: "This soft, luxurious bathmat and matching lid cover are tufted with DuPont nylon. They are described as machine washable by the manufacturer and since nylon does not absorb dirt, should stay clean longer. The Neddletuft mats and covers are usually fluffy and come in array of glowing colors all with an attractive faint sheen imparted by the frosty looking nylon fibers." (Brand spelled "Neddletuft" in the catalogue; probably "Needletuft", not confirmed.) Catalogue title is "Nylon carpeting"; bracketed words added to tell it from 1972341_1333. Why it matters: tufted bath mats were a main product of the Dalton-style tufting industry between bedspreads and broadloom; this shows DuPont promoting nylon for tufted mats around 1955 (Hagley's approximate date).
