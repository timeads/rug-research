Research notes

Khamis returned to Egypt in 1978 at the invitation of Sadat's privatising government and started with a single loom in 1979. Mainly machine-woven rugs; its tufted arm is MAC Carpet (Misr American Carpets), established 1981. US arm in Dalton from 1993; listed on the Egyptian Exchange 1997; China unit 2006. Khamis died in 2020. Why it matters: described as the Middle East's largest machine-made rug producer; mostly woven, with tufting as a separate line. Dates: 1979 (company, Forbes ME) vs 1980 (Domotex). "Largest producer of machine-woven and tufted rugs" and "global leader in tufted" are company claims.
