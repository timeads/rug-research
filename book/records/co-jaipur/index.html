Research notes

Started with 2 looms and 9 artisans; built a village artisan network of about 40,000. US headquarters in Atlanta from 1998, led by Asha Chaudhary. Core business is hand-knotted, but it also makes hand-tufted rugs (gun-tufted through a pre-woven cloth with a cotton-latex backing) and power-loomed lines. Bridges the India chapter and the US market. Dates: 1978 confirmed; 1998 Atlanta is a company claim; starting capital Rs 5,000 vs $200.
