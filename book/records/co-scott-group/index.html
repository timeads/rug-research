Research notes

Moved to Grand Rapids in the late 1970s; renamed The Scott Group in 1982; bought after Sellinger's death (1995) by Michael Ruggeri, Richard Ruggeri and Timothy Hill. Made the 2010 Oval Office rug. Acquired Hokanson (hand-knotted) and PWV Studios (hand-tufted, founded 1983 by V'Soske). More than 50 tufting artisans in Grand Rapids. Dates: 1969 confirmed; Hokanson acquisition 2014 vs 2017; "one of the largest US hand-tufted makers" is marketing.
