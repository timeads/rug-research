Research notes

This is a yarn-feed and tension device (bracket, gun head, roller frame, feed rollers) for automated gun-tufting machines, meant to stop yarn tangling. Its background cites CN207313899U, a synchronous yarn feeder for a gun-tufting carpet machine, which shows a cluster of 2017–2020 patents on robot gun-tufting. It is included as an example of that cluster, not because it is important on its own.
