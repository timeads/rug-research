Research notes

Prof. 孙以泽 (Sun Yize), now an academician; 2nd Class award for digital tufted carpet loom equipment. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): confirmed. Official project title: 簇绒地毯织机系列成套装备技术及其产业化 (complete tufted-carpet loom equipment series and its industrialisation), National S&T Progress Award, 2nd class, 2010. A 2019 Donghua article calls it the 数字化簇绒地毯织机系列成套装备 project. Sun is styled 院士 (academician) in Donghua pages from 2023 onward. He won a second 2nd-class national award in 2018, for braiding equipment. Source: 孙以泽 (Sun Yize) profile, 东华大学 Donghua University, 2010 award.
