Research notes

Hand-knotted context. Passavant, a Swiss Huguenot, bought Parisot's workshop (sources say 1755 or 1756) and moved the weavers to Exeter. Huguenots of Spitalfields: only three Passavant carpets survive (V&A, Petworth House, private collection); its page features the 1758 Petworth carpet. The NPS notes Exeter carpets' 'counting warps' and a signature and date woven into the border. Axminster Heritage: Exeter Carpets stopped trading in 1761 because the carpets were too expensive. Conflict: one source places all three survivors in English country houses; the Cambridge History of Western Textiles reportedly credits Winterthur for an image of a 1757 Passavant carpet (see wint-pre-wilton-leads).
