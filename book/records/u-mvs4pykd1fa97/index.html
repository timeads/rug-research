Research notes

Trade catalogue. WinterCat: New York, W. and J. Sloane, [c1910]; 24 p., col. ill.; "Distributed by W. and J. Sloane." Why it matters: Woven carpet / Processes: "chenille Axminster" is a woven chenille-weft carpet, not tufted; useful to distinguish chenille carpet from Dalton "chenille" bedspreads. Tagged possible.
