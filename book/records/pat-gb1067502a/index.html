Research notes

This is a British-originated tufting-machine patent from Singer-Cobble Ltd, Blackburn, on needle and looper operation (inferred from keywords). It is good evidence that the British arm of Cobble filed its own machine inventions in the 1960s. Grace's Guide says the American parent was taken over by Singer in 1960 and the Blackburn subsidiary became Singer-Cobble. Edgar Pickering left Singer-Cobble to found Edgar Pickering (Blackburn) in 1964, and Ronald Ellison founded Ellison Tufting Machinery in 1961; I found no patents for either.
