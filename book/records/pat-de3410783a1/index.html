Research notes

This is a hand-tufting device from Akzo's German arm (Wuppertal, the former Enka/Glanzstoff fibre business). It suggests that fibre makers took an interest in hand guns in the 1980s, probably for sampling or repair. It cites Scholz's DE2356585.
