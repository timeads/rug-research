Research notes

The definitive Japanese-language source, from hand-woven dantsu to the present. ¥5,000 including shipping; order from the association, 06-6809-2868. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): confirmed. Quote and price (税込5,000円, 送料込み) are confirmed; the price page is dated around Jan 2023. Add: it was published April 2008 as the 50th-anniversary project of 日本特殊毛織物等工業組合. The phone number 06-6809-2868 was not verified. Source: 組合発行書籍のご案内 (Association publications) – 日本カーペット工業組合, April 2008.
