Research notes

Research proposal, 1952, by Ernest Dichter for the Carpet Institute, Inc., on "the Sales and Advertising Problems of the Carpet Institute". File-level entry only; not digitised as far as found. Why it matters: the Carpet Institute was the wool-carpet industry's trade body; a 1952 study of why carpet was not selling sets the scene for nylon and tufted carpet later in the decade, and pairs with Dichter's 1957-58 DuPont nylon carpet studies already in the database. Found: Hagley finding aids, search "Carpet Institute", 2026-10-28.
