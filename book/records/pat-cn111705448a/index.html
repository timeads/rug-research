Research notes

This is a pneumatic tufting gun designed as the end-effector of a tufting robot, from a large Shandong carpet group. It is close kin to the Hofmann/Scholz pneumatic hand gun and Wilcom's pneumatic head. A companion utility model, CN212426405U ("pneumatic multifunctional carpet pricking gun for an intelligent robot"), appears in the same similar-documents lists, but I did not see its date. Another background citation, CN108642747A (an intelligent robot gun-tufting device with screw-driven XYZ motion, cited in CN110468525A), was seen only secondhand.
