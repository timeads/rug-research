Research notes

The first tufted product was in carded wool ("moquette en laine cardée"); synthetics came later. Caveat: 1954 is when Balsan took over the firm; no French source dates the first machine or first metre. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): confirmed in a different source. The quoted sentence is from French Wikipedia, not the heritage association. AMSI-Balsan independently says Louis brought tufting back from the US to make 'moquette en laine cardée', with synthetics later. AMSI frames 1954–73 as Louis holding the Direction Générale under the presidency of his cousin Jean de Layre, which differs from 'succeeded François'. The fr.wikipedia company article has a section 'La reconversion de 1955', and Balsan's own catalogue says Louis introduced tufting in 1954. Production ran on 5-m-wide machines described as unique in France. There is still no dated first machine or first metre. Source: Louis Balsan (1911-1982) – Wikipédia (French).
