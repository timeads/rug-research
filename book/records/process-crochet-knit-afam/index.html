Research notes

How it works: strips of cloth or yarn are worked with a crochet hook into chained loops, built row by row or in rounds into a dense mat with no separate foundation. This object: crocheted from hand-dyed wool, woven wool cloth and recycled ingrain carpet, 55 × 37 1/2 in. The record is marked as a work in progress. Knitting: AFAM's exhibition 'The Great Cover-Up' (2007) showed a rare knitted rug attributed to Elvira Hulett of the Hancock Shaker community (object number not seen). Other crochet: a Wichita Art Museum hooked rug of about 1880 has a crocheted edge. No earlier dated crocheted or knitted floor rug was found.
