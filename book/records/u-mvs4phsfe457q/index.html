"Diploma awarded by Mechanics Fair Boston, 1878, for the best and handsomest patterns manufactured." Subjects include Rugs, Hooked -- Equipment and supplies -- Catalogs. Why it matters: Hooked rugs: a dated Frost catalogue (1884), with equipment, for the stencilled-pattern trade that preceded the punch and tufting tools.
