Research notes

This patent puts the hand tufting gun on a computer-guided gantry. It covers a single hollow-needle tufting head moved over the backing from vector design files, with a yarn cutter that switches between cut and loop pile. Its background is a useful history: it says tufting guns "may be controlled and guided manually by a human operator" and names earlier hand guns, Hartleb (DE2815801) and Scholz (US3968758/DE2356585). US counterpart: US7218987B2 (granted 15 May 2007). Austrian member: ATE522650T1. Wilcom (Sydney, best known for embroidery software) had earlier tufting-head patents, US5829372 and US5503092 (Wilcom Tufting Pty Ltd). Their background cites PCT/AU92/00401 (WO 93/03215), and there are Chinese (CN1046772C), Australian (AU1058695A) and Japanese (JPH09505361A) members. I saw those numbers but not their dates.
