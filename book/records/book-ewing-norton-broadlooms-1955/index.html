Research notes

Bigelow-Sanford's history, written from the company's records: the main secondary source for Bigelow's power looms and for broadloom (open question: Bigelow's first broadloom, 1877, and when "broadloom" came into use). Access (checked Oct 2026): scanned on the Internet Archive but restricted to print-disabled readers; otherwise a library copy (Open Library: https://openlibrary.org/works/OL160421W). Cited in Deaton, Bedspreads to Broadloom (1993), bibliography pp. 209–217 (transcribed in research/deaton-sources.md).
