Research notes

Tim Eads, a founder of Tuft the World and the author of this history, stated on 31 October 2026 that: Trish Andersen was already tufting when he started, and the two became friends remotely and shared information back and forth; Unnecessary Inventions uses Tuft the World supplies and is making a line of pieces based on the Pantone colour book; and Tuft the World has collaborated with Qualeasha Wood on some work. He also described David Nott's carving and expertise, Simji as an influencer who promotes tufting to a mass audience, and Melissa Monroe's three-dimensional pieces. Source: Tim Eads, in conversation while drafting this history, 31 Oct 2026. First-hand knowledge; the author's interest should be noted when citing it.
