Research notes

What it shows: the earliest firm, well-known group of decorated felts. Britannica (Scythian art) says felt appliqué wall hangings were found at Pazyryk, some with the Great Goddess or part-human beasts and others with geometric or animal motifs. Wikipedia says the best-known figural hangings (an investiture scene and a part-human, part-bird creature) are in the Hermitage. The University of Washington Silk Road page on the Hermitage notes saddlecloths and cushions with appliqué felt, dyed fur and embroidery. A Hermitage curator's tour report also mentions felt swan figures. Felt here is cut and sewn appliqué on felt grounds, not woven. Conflicts: the Hermitage listing dates Barrow 5 to 252–238 BCE, which is later than Rudenko's original dates, and other sources give 5th–4th or 4th–3rd century BCE. An Art Resource image calls the Hermitage 'Horseman' felt a 'carpet detail', but a blog calls it a wall hanging.
