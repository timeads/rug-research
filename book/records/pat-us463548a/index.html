Research notes

Checked against the USPTO document: E. Ross, "Turfing Implement", No. 463,548, patented November 17, 1891. The drawing shows a two-handled needle-and-gauge tool of the same form as the Susan Burr. "Turfing" is the patent's own word. Expired 1908; cited in 1948 by Shaycraft (US2442906A). Filed 15 July 1891 by Ebenezer Ross of Toledo, Ohio, as an improvement on his own US251381 ("Embroidery Machine", 1881).
