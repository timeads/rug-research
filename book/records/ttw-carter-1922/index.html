Research notes

The machine line starts from sewing machines, not from the old hand tools. Patent found: US1485213, August J. Carter, Chattanooga, "Tufting-Attachment Mechanism for Sewing Machines", filed 21 Jul 1922, granted 26 Feb 1924.
