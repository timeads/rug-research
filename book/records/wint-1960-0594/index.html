Research notes

Bequest of Henry Francis du Pont. Laurel Thatcher Ulrich links it to an "E.F." bed rug (Elizabeth Foote) at the Connecticut Historical Society and an unmarked rug at Historic Deerfield; all three may commemorate the Foot siblings' marriages on 5 November 1778. A dated, named, pre-1800 needle-made pile textile at Winterthur: a strong anchor object for a tufting-lineage argument.
