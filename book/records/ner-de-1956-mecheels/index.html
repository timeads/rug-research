Research notes

The 1956 sentence is confirmed on German Wikipedia, and bwd 1959 independently supports about 1956 ("gut drei Jahre" of industrial use). But no library record for the Mecheels book was found, and the footnote linking the sentence to it wasn't seen. The printed source behind German Wikipedia's "In Deutschland wurde 1956 mit der industriellen Fertigung von Tufting-Erzeugnissen begonnen". Not online; p. 475 is one page to photograph. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): partly confirmed. The Wikipedia sentence 'In Deutschland wurde 1956 mit der industriellen Fertigung von „Tufting-Erzeugnissen“ begonnen' is confirmed on DeWiki. NOT verified: that it is footnoted to Mecheels/Vogler/Kurz. No library or catalogue record for 'Kultur- & Industriegeschichte der Textilien' (Wachter, Bönnigheim 2009, ISBN 978-3-9812485-3-1) surfaced. Independent support for ~1956: bwd 1959 ('gut drei Jahre' of industrial use; Dura founded about 3 years earlier). Groz-Beckert dates European industrial tufting to 1955. Baunetz Wissen says 'Anfang der 1950er'. Source: Tufting – DeWiki (mirror of German Wikipedia 'Tufting').
