Research notes

A hollow-needle tufting machine for patterned articles in several yarn colours. Knives move between no-cut and cut positions to make loop and cut pile (Justia abstract). Card-Monroe's filings say earlier hollow-needle machines (the Kile, Davis and Tapistron line) used solenoid-controlled air cylinders to pick colours, but needle spacing limited how many colours each needle could take. A continuation is US12546042B2 (appl. 18/955,318), and a later EP application covers artificial turf. Card-Monroe's company timeline lists 'Hollow Needle 2023' (see co-card-monroe), and the August 2023 filing fits it, but no launch news was found. Why it matters: in 2023 the leading broadloom builder returned to the hollow-needle, individually fed yarn idea that Tapistron pioneered around 1990.
