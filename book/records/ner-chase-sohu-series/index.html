Research notes

Includes the German firm 鲁麟洋行, "the earliest exporter of Chinese carpets". Sohu blocks bots. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): confirmed. The series exists, with episodes including 03：现代地毯业肇始河西 and 04：从地毯图案看天津地毯的历史变迁. 鲁麟洋行 is Wm. Pustau & Co., founded at Canton in 1845 by the Hamburg merchant Pustau; it reached Shanghai in 1855 and opened a Tianjin branch in 1890. It was an order-placing house, not a manufacturer. The 'earliest exporter' claim rests on an anecdote about a Pustau manager seeing carpets at the Yonghegong. Sohu pages appear in search results; direct fetching was not possible here. Source: 05：最早出口中国地毯的鲁麟洋行 (Episode 05: Pustau & Co., the earliest exporter of Chinese carpets), Sohu.
