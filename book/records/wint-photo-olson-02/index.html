Research notes

Stamped burlap patterns (Quaint Fawn, Wild Rose Bouquet, Pleasing Cosmos, Terrier Pillow) with colour charts; "How To Make Hook Rugs" instructions; So. E. Z. Automatic Needle, 25¢, "Hi-Speed works like magic". Sold in S. S. Kresge stores; mail orders to Wonder Art, 3900 N. Claremont Ave, Chicago. Note: the pages name Wonder Art, not Olson. Photographed by Tuft the World at Winterthur, January 2023; the photo is in your Google Drive (private link).
