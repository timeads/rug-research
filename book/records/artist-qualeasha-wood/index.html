Research notes

A textile artist whose tufted and jacquard-woven tapestries combine craft with digital imagery. Her 2023 exhibition "TL;DR" at Pippy Houldsworth Gallery, London, included tufted works in wool and acrylic, among them "Into the Blue" (2023, 50 by 64 in.), a figure in a bath among pink tiles and rubber ducks, and "All You Must Hold On To" (2023, 66 by 68 in.) (Hypebae, June 2023). The Metropolitan Museum acquired her woven tapestry The [Black] Madonna/Whore Complex (2021). Artist in residence at the Studio Museum in Harlem, 2021–2022. Instagram: @qualeasha (artist biography; WePresent). Research note (Oct 2026): from search results; pages not opened.
