Research notes

A modern Brussels-loom sample, given by the makers, after a design by the Spitalfields silk designer Anna Maria Garthwaite dated 1753. The weave is modern, but the record shows a mid-18th-century design woven in the Brussels technique of the period. Garthwaite's dates differ between sources (c.1688 or 1690 to 1763). Sources: https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O258637/carpet-sample-anna-maria-garthwaite · https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/blue-plaques/anna-maria-garthwaite/ Research note (Oct 2026): from search results; pages not opened.
