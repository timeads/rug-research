Research notes

Catherine Evans Whitener's own account of making the first tufted spread (1895) and selling it (1900): the founding story first-hand, 40 years on. Access: not online as far as checked (Oct 2026). Dalton newspapers on microfilm: try the Dalton-Whitfield public library and Crown Gardens & Archives, Dalton (named in Deaton's bibliography for another Dalton News article). Cited in Deaton, Bedspreads to Broadloom (1993), bibliography pp. 209–217 (transcribed in research/deaton-sources.md).
