Research notes

Grandson 藤本荘太郎, the 緞通王 ("dantsu king"), 「渡米して海外への普及に努めた」 travelled to America to spread it abroad, seventy years before the tufting machine came the other way. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): confirmed. None. The same city page contains '庄左衛門の孫で「緞通王」とよばれた藤本荘太郎が技術改良を加え、渡米して海外への普及に努めた', and says Meiji-era exports flourished. 'Seventy years before the tufting machine' is the brief's own arithmetic. Source: 堺式手織緞通 (Sakai-style hand-woven dantsu) – 堺市 (City of Sakai) cultural-properties page.
