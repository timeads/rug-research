Research notes

Issued 20 Oct 1989, in force 1 Jul 1990. The hardest date available: a country does not write a national standard for a novelty, so real production was running for years before 1989. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): confirmed. Dates are confirmed. Additional details: the main drafter was 天津地毯研究所 (Tianjin Carpet Research Institute), under the national technical committee for arts and crafts (TC150) and the China National Light Industry Council. It was replaced in full by GB/T 11746-2008 (issued 2008-06-16, effective 2009-03-01). The original 1989 PDF is headed 'GB 11746-89', so the '/T' was probably applied later. The 'production running for years before 1989' line is the brief's inference. Source: 簇绒地毯 GB/T 11746-1989 (Tufted carpet), 全国标准信息公共服务平台 (National Public Service Platform for Standards Information), Issued 1989-10-20; implemented 1990-07-01; abolished 2009-03-01.
