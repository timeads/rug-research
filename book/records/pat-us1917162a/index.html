Checked against the USPTO document: L. P. Smith, "Motor Operated Tufting Machine", No. 1,917,162, filed June 23, 1932, granted July 4, 1933. A handheld tool with the motor in the body and the needle below: the earliest motorised hand tufting tool in this database. Inventor Lawrence P. Smith of Dalton; assigned to Cabin Crafts.
