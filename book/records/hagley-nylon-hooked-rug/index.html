Research notes

Hooked rug, 116 × 139 in. (Hagley museum record), in nylon, linked to DuPont's wear-testing of nylon for floor coverings; it frayed and pilled when walked on. Shown in the 2015 "Unraveling Stories" exhibit. Probably the only nylon hooked rug: a direct bridge between handmade rugs and nylon carpet. Found via search-engine results (Hagley's site blocks automated reading): open the link to confirm and see the image. Museum record: https://museumcollection.hagley.org/objects/40662/hooked-rug
