Research notes

Carpet Day was established in 1974 by the carpet industry association. The Emperor and Empress toured Suminoe's Sumiyoshi plant on 1 Nov 1956 and viewed tufted products. Commemorates 1 Nov 1956, when the Shōwa Emperor and Empress toured Suminoe's Sumiyoshi plant. Citation check (Oct 2026, from search results; pages not opened): confirmed. Add: Carpet Day was established in 1974 by the carpet industry association (日本カーペット工業組合, per Wikipedia; probably its predecessor body at the time). The Emperor AND Empress (行幸啓) visited Suminoe's Sumiyoshi plant on 1 Nov 1956 and viewed tufted products. Can be upgraded from 'suggestive'.
