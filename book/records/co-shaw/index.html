Research notes

Began dyeing small tufted goods (bedspreads, scatter rugs) during Dalton's chenille boom, then became the world's largest commission finisher of tufted carpet as Star Finishing Co. A 1967 holding company bought Philadelphia Carpet Co. (founded 1846), Shaw's first move into making carpet; public as Shaw Industries in 1971; first yarn plant 1972; acquisitions included West Point-Pepperell's carpet business (1987), Salem (1992) and Queen (1999). Owned by Berkshire Hathaway since 2001. Shaw Rugs also made licensed "Winterthur Collection" nylon area rugs (see that record). Dates: 1946 Star Dye confirmed; Star Finishing 1958 (Shaw) vs 1960 (Georgia Encyclopedia); 1967 is the holding company, not the origin. "World's largest carpet manufacturer" dates from 1987; don't repeat it as current.
