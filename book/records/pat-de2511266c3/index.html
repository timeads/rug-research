Research notes

This is Scholz's second hand-tufting patent family, filed while Scholz Handtuft-Technik KG was operating. Family members: AT347214B ("Vorrichtung zum Tuften von Teppichen od. dgl.", D05C15/06), US4029031A ("Device for providing rugs and the like with tufts", 14 Jun 1977, inventor Hartmut Scholz), GB1536346A, FR2303888A1, IT1063070B and a Japanese member JPS51116752A. One summary of US4029031 describes a manual tufting device with a fixed needle, a movable cutting blade and two arms that slide against each other. This is consistent with the hand-operated (later pneumatic) guns that Hofmann still sells, but I have not read the claims.
