Research notes

The best-documented hobby and studio robot-tufting build. Trueblood's Hackaday.io log (February 2022) shows how he replaced a consumer tufting gun's 555-timer PWM motor controller with an Arduino. He added a 3.5 mm jack carrying an on/off input and an analogue speed input so a robot can drive the gun, and he explains the gun's loop- and cut-pile modes. Hackaday covered it on 5 Mar 2022 as 'Robotic Tufting Gun Fires Off CNC Textiles', calling it the slow, arbitrary-pattern kind of machine, unlike fast fixed-pattern industrial tufters. A collaboration write-up, 'Parks x Needle', says the gun went on a UR5 robot arm, after a first try with a punch needle was dropped for speed. It tufted the shapes of NYC park wildlife zones, with hand punch-needling to fill in, for the Ground Truth IRL exhibition (April 2023).
