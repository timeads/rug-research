Research notes

A 144-page beginners' manual by the tufting artist and content creator SIMJI: tools, a warm-up project and step-by-step projects, with QR codes linking to her YouTube tutorials (reviews). The publisher calls her the "rugfluencer" and says she shares her tufting videos with millions of viewers on TikTok, YouTube and other platforms. Born in South Korea and raised in Los Angeles.
