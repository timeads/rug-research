## Gaps
- No digitized carpet ads before 1967 surfaced: nothing for "501", Cumuloft or Caprolan at Hagley.
- No carpet photographs surfaced in Acc. 1984.259 (Textile Fibers product photos): only hosiery and apparel are indexed.
- A "Step Out on Color" (1960) nylon-carpet film appeared in one AI-generated summary only. Treat it as unconfirmed.

## To request or check in person (askhagley@hagley.org)
1. Acc. 1803 advertising tearsheet boxes for **1958–1966** (Box 43 and nearby), for "501" and "carpets of DuPont nylon" ads. The digitized set is partial.
2. Acc. 1995.300 finding aid: search "carpet"; confirm *The Opening Door* (1959) and other DuPont Carpet Nylon commercials.
3. Undigitized parts of Acc. 1984.259 (Product Information textile files, bulk 1955–79) and the Textile Fibers Dept. pamphlet series (findingaids.hagley.org/repositories/3/resources/1278).
4. Fiber labels on William Pahlmann's carpet samples (Acc. 2388); BBDO "DuPont – Nylon, 1941–1966" films (cold storage).
5. Nylon hooked rug 2012.8: ask to view it and its wear-testing documentation.
