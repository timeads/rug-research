Abstract
The utility model relates to a hand held lateral electric knitting needle for knitting carpet, comprising a needle nozzle, ream scissors, a needle frame steel jacket, a needle head, a nozzle internal sleeve, a scissors internal sleeve, a copper outer plate, a movable connecting rod, a gear box and a miniature electric motor arranged in an electric motor casing. The needle nozzle and the ream scissors can be laterally arranged in the needle frame steel jacket and can laterally move via the specially designed nozzle internal sleeve and the scissors internal sleeve arranged in the needle head when knitting blanket. Therefore, various primary materials such as polypropylene fiber, real silk, real silk mixing velvet, rayon and yarn can be knitted into 1 to 8 velvet wires in order to increase the quality and the efficiency of weaving blanket. The knitted velvet surface is smooth, the length difference of the velvet is small, so the utility model can save a large amount of primary materials and reduce the cost.
