# Winterthur: nylon rugs, hooked-rug ephemera, pattern books and samples

*Checked October 2026. Finding aids were read in full by the research-fetch workflow (copies in `research/fetched/`). The museum catalog's old static index returns "410 Page No Longer Available", so the page-by-page crawl found nothing. The museum answers below come from search-engine results for museumcollection.winterthur.org.*

## Does the museum have nylon rugs?
Nothing turned up. Every Winterthur rug record that search surfaces is wool, cotton, linen or burlap, and all of them are already in the database (object/28352, 25738, 1923, 42781, 26610, 25734 and others). The only "Winterthur" nylon rug found is a **licensed reproduction**, a 100% nylon "Winterthur Collection" rug sold by Shaw Rugs (record `wint-shaw-licensed-nylon`). That is a product, not a collection object.
To close this properly, ask the registrar or curator to search the catalog's materials field for nylon, acrylic, polyester and olefin.

The nylon hooked rugs are at **Hagley**:
- 2012.8.1, by Nancy du Pont Reynolds Cooch, 1950, 116 × 139 in.
- 2015.10.2, maker unknown, 1975–90, nylon and cotton, 23 × 35 in. Newly added.

## Hooked-rug ads, pattern books and samples in the library
| Record | What the finding aid says |
|---|---|
| Laura M. Allen collection, Col. 979 | **acc. 2017x86.1 "Hooked Rugs": scrapbook of articles, pictures and advertisements pertaining to hooked rugs, 1920s.** Also a 1911 rug notebook (hooked rugs "taught by Mrs. W. E. Raymond, Home Bureau member, Rochester"), hooked-rug clippings, and a Mary M. Atwater article on rug weaving. |
| E. Ross & Co., [Catalog of rug patterns], Toledo, 1891 | A hooked-rug pattern catalog, digitised on archive.org. Ross also patented the hooking tool (US 251,381; US 463,548). |
| Swatch Book Collection, Col. 50 | Mostly dress and furnishing cloth. Carpet item: **Tariff Manufacturing Co., "Brussels colours dyed at Tariffville, Conn.", c. 1840**, two dye-recipe notebooks for Brussels carpet (2017x85.30a-b). No hooked or tufted samples are listed. |
| Digital collections: "Weaving Drafts and Pattern Books", "Swatch Books and Salesmen's Sample Books" | Weaving, knitting and embroidery pattern books from the 18th and 19th centuries. The landing pages list no rug titles; browse the items. |

## What to ask the library (Downs Collection, reference desk)
1. Scans of the advertisements in the Allen "Hooked Rugs" scrapbook (2017x86.1).
2. A trade-catalogue search for: hooked rug patterns (Ross, Frost, Garrett/Bluenose, Diamond Dye), rug hooks, chenille and candlewick bedspreads, tufted rugs, and carpet sample books (Bigelow, Mohawk, Alexander Smith).
3. Whether the printed-ephemera files hold Home Bureau or extension-service hooked-rug pamphlets.
