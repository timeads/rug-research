Every claim below carries the original-language text, a translation, a source, and a confidence level.

THE HEADLINE FINDINGS  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. NO COUNTRY CLAIMS TO HAVE INVENTED TUFTING. Every non-English national record — Chinese, Japanese,  
   German, French, Dutch — describes tufting as something imported from America. Searching in five  
   languages did not turn up a single independent inventor anywhere. That is a much stronger version  
   of the claim than we could make from English sources alone.

2. EVERYWHERE, TUFTING WAS ADOPTED BY OUTSIDERS, NOT BY THE ESTABLISHED WEAVERS. The German trade  
   press said so explicitly in 1959. Japan shows the same pattern. So does Britain. This is a real  
   cross-cultural finding and nobody seems to have written it down.

3. "CHINESE ART DECO" AND "NICHOLS RUGS" DO NOT EXIST IN CHINESE. Searched specifically. Zero  
   Chinese-language sources use those categories. They are Anglophone collector-market inventions.  
   The Chinese tell the same period as industrial and labour history — factory counts, export tonnage,  
   named merchants. Americans assume these are the same story. They are not.

4. CHINA BUILT ITS FIRST DOMESTIC TUFTING MACHINE IN 2004. The country with the oldest carpet  
   tradition on earth was the last major producer to build the machine.

5. THE INDIA DATE GAP IS REAL, AND I CAN NOW PROVE IT. Not a search failure.

── WHEN DID TUFTING ARRIVE? Genuinely undocumented. Here are the anchors. ──

"机制地毯自上世纪70年代在中国开始大规模生产以来"  
"Since machine-made carpet began large-scale production in China in the 1970s..."  
— Sina republication of trade article. SUGGESTIVE. Only explicit dating statement found in Chinese.

GB/T 11746-1989「簇绒地毯」— national standard for TUFTED CARPET, issued 1989-10-20, in force 1990-07-01.  
— 全国标准信息公共服务平台 (State Administration for Market Regulation) database. SOLID.  
This is the hardest date available. A country does not write a national standard for a novelty —  
so real production was running for years before 1989.

"北京燕山石油化工公司地毯厂，是我国最早的机织地毯生产企业...中国现代化地毯工业的摇篮"  
"The Beijing Yanshan Petrochemical Company Carpet Factory was China's earliest machine-woven carpet  
enterprise... the cradle of China's modernised carpet industry."  
— 中国税务杂志社. SUGGESTIVE (company profile).  
NOTE: China's carpet modernisation was led by an OIL REFINERY. It's a downstream petrochemical  
story, not a textile-mill story.

── CHINA BUILT ITS OWN TUFTING MACHINE ONLY IN 2004 ──

"簇绒装备技术基本掌握在欧美几大公司手中，如美国CMC、Tuftco、Cobble等"  
"Tufting equipment technology was basically held by a few European and American companies —  
America's CMC, Tuftco, Cobble."

"上海华源集团地毯有限公司委托东华大学机械学院研制开发了DHT－200地毯簇绒机，在整机产品设计、  
制造生产上均填补了国内空白"  
"Shanghai Huayuan Group Carpet Co. commissioned Donghua University's School of Mechanical  
Engineering to develop the DHT-200 carpet tufting machine, which FILLED A DOMESTIC BLANK in both  
whole-machine design and manufacturing." (2004)  
— 《纺织导报》 Textile Leader. SOLID (trade-technical journal).  
填补国内空白 is a standard Chinese formula meaning: nobody in China could do this before.

Prof. 孙以泽 (Sun Yize)'s Donghua team won the 2010 NATIONAL SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY PROGRESS AWARD,  
2nd Class, for digital tufted carpet loom equipment. He is now an academician.  
— 东华大学 official site. SOLID.

AND CHINA STILL IMPORTS THE HIGH END. Customs HS 84479011「簇绒地毯织机」, March 2025 alone:  
Jiangsu imported one machine at US$665,441; Zhejiang one at US$471,660. SOLID (customs data).

Chinese patents tell the same story — the significant tufting machine filings IN China are FOREIGN:  
CN113584750A (Card Monroe Corp, US, filed 2017); CN1030783C (Norddeutsche Faserwerke, Germany, 1992).  
The 1990s Chinese carpet patents are about improving HAND looms.

── THE CHINESE CARPET LINEAGE RUNS THROUGH TIBET, NOT PERSIA ──

The standard Chinese account of the Beijing commercial trade begins in 1860, when a Tibetan lama  
came to Beijing with carpets for the Xianfeng Emperor and established production at 报国寺 (Baoguo  
Temple) near 广安门, founding two schools: 「东门法」 and 「西门法」 (Eastern Gate method / Western  
Gate method). SUGGESTIVE (popular history, widely repeated).

Official intangible-heritage listing for 北京宫毯织造技艺 (Beijing palace carpet weaving):  
"织毯技术西汉时由西域传入我国，到唐代，中原地区的地毯织造已大致成熟"  
"Carpet weaving entered China from the Western Regions in the Western Han; by the Tang, weaving in  
the Central Plains was largely mature." 1900 gold medal, Paris Exposition. 1920: 354 workshops.  
1956: Beijing No.1 Carpet Factory (joint state-private).  
CURRENT STATUS: "后继乏人，织造技艺面临失传的危险" — "no successors; the technique faces the danger  
of being lost."  
— 中国非物质文化遗产网 (state heritage registry). SOLID.

── BEIJING AND TIANJIN, THE EXPORT ERA ──

1920: 354 factories in Beijing, \~5,000 workers.  
1926: carpet exports peak, a 115-fold increase over 1912.  
Foreign control: American businessmen F.C. FETTE and A. SHOEMAKER dominated. Fette partnered with  
Chinese merchant 李立轩 to found 仰经地毯厂, controlling \~1/3 of output by 1924, 1,200+ workers.  
Labour: 2–3 yuan/month, 9–16 hour days, two meals of steamed bread and vegetables, apprentices  
unpaid and beaten. SUGGESTIVE (popular history column).

Tianjin in the 1930s: 303 factories and workshops, 2,749 looms, 11,568 workers. Export volume once  
70–80% of the national total. The 风船 ("sailing ship") brand became internationally known.  
Gold medals at Leipzig 1965 and 1986.  
DECLINE: "1990年代后…城市发展使主要工厂迁移" — after the 1990s, urban development displaced the  
major factories. Tianjin's hand-knotting industry died of REAL ESTATE, not competition.  
— 天津日报 (Tianjin Daily) + trade sources. SOLID/SUGGESTIVE.

── THE HONG KONG RECORD ON TAI PING — RICHER IN CHINESE THAN IN ENGLISH ──

This is the best find. The Hong Kong Heritage Project (Kadoorie family archive, independent):

"工程師出身的太平總經理葉元章參考了美國用單針衣車造地氈的方法和家用簇絨工具，再加點創新的  
工程思維，開發了新的割絨織針。"  
"Tai Ping's general manager Yip Yuen-cheung [Anthony Yeh], trained as an engineer, referred to THE  
AMERICAN METHOD OF MAKING CARPET WITH A SINGLE-NEEDLE SEWING MACHINE **AND TO DOMESTIC HAND-TUFTING  
TOOLS**, added some innovative engineering thinking, and developed a new cut-pile tufting needle."

"1973年為簇絨槍裝上摩打，並且減輕重量。"  
"In 1973 a motor was fitted to the tufting gun, and its weight was reduced."

"1992年7月，所有地氈生產遷往南海"  
"In July 1992 all carpet production moved to Nanhai [Guangdong]."  
— hongkongheritage.org. SOLID.

WHY THIS MATTERS: the Chinese text says Yeh worked from the American industrial method AND from  
DOMESTIC HAND-TUFTING TOOLS — i.e. from exactly the kind of object you're holding. Your Susan Burr  
and the Dalton machine are BOTH named as sources for the Hong Kong tool. The English-language  
version of this story does not make that second half explicit.

── TERMINOLOGY (sharper in Chinese than in English) ──

簇绒       tufting (generic process — yarn punched through backing, latex applied)  
簇绒机     the industrial broadloom tufting machine  
簇绒枪     tufting gun (the handheld tool; HK/Cantonese usage)  
枪刺 / 刺枪  "gun-stab" — THE everyday Chinese trade word for hand-tufting. More common than 手工簇绒.  
手工枪刺地毯  hand gun-tufted carpet (the commercial product category)  
手工打结    hand-knotted (the prestige category)  
机织       machine-woven — Wilton 威尔顿, Axminster 阿克明斯特  
针刺       needle-punched (needlefelt — separate, cheaper)  
栽绒       "planted pile" — older/archaeological term for knotted pile  
地毯 / 地氈  mainland vs Hong Kong written forms  
戳戳绣/戳戳秀 "poke-poke embroidery" — the hobby-craze name on Chinese social media

THE SAME SALES TRICK IN TWO LANGUAGES: Chinese marketing drops 胶背 ("glue-back") and sells the  
product as 手工枪刺地毯, so consumers hear 手工 ("handmade") and infer hand-knotted. Identical to  
what "hand-tufted" does in English.

── SURPRISES ──

• CUIHUANGKOU, TIANJIN (崔黄口镇) is officially named alongside Dalton and Gaziantep as one of the  
  world's three great carpet clusters. Under 100 km², \~50,000 residents, 1,200+ carpet enterprises.  
  "国内每十张地毯，八张来自崔黄口" — eight of every ten Chinese carpets come from there. Nearly half  
  of national output. You've heard of Dalton. Almost no American has heard of Cuihuangkou. SOLID.

• CHINA IS THE WORLD'S #1 CARPET EXPORTER (23.5% of world share, 2023\) AND ITS BIGGEST CUSTOMER IS  
  IRAQ, NOT AMERICA. Tianjin, Jan–Oct 2024: Iraq 19.6%, USA 17.1%, Malaysia 11.2%. SOLID.

• WHAT KILLED CHINESE HAND-WEAVING WASN'T TUFTING — IT WAS HEAT-TRANSFER PRINTING (热转印).  
  "现在我们一个月生产五六十万平米，以前得干10年" — "Now we produce 500,000–600,000 m² a month;  
  before, that took ten years." The Chinese arc is hand → gun → machine → PRINTING. SOLID.

• NINGXIA WAS THE IMPERIAL RUG SOURCE. Of the 1,000+ carpets in the Palace Museum collection,  
  Ningxia carpets are MORE THAN 50%. SOLID (state heritage registry).

• [CORRECTION: Tai Ping motorised its gun in 1973; US patents for motorised handheld tufting tools date from 1932. See the database record "CONFLICT: when was the tufting gun invented?"] THE TUFTING GUN WAS INVENTED IN HONG KONG IN 1973 AND RE-ENTERED MAINLAND CHINA AROUND 2021 AS AN  
  IMPORTED WESTERN TIKTOK TREND, under the name 戳戳秀 ("poke-poke"). A Chengdu shop owner:  
  "一个月前在抖音上看到有人玩戳戳秀，觉得确实解压，立刻开起铺子" — "a month ago I saw people doing  
  poke-poke on Douyin, thought it really relieved stress, and immediately opened a shop." SOLID.

• CHINESE HAND-KNOTTERS DESCRIBE MACHINE CARPET AS THEIR OWN DESCENDANT: "现代机械地毯技术是从手工  
  打结技术演化而来" — "modern machine carpet technology evolved out of hand-knotting." A far more  
  generous framing than the Western "machines destroyed the craft." SUGGESTIVE.

── PANIPAT IS NOT AN OLD RUG TOWN ──

The carpet skill came from UTTAR PRADESH, not from Pakistan:  
"The urbanisation of this industry along with its export worthiness has seen the spread of this  
industry to Panipat in the state of Haryana, WHERE THE WEAVERS MIGRATED FROM BHADOHI-MIRZAPUR."  
— CUTS International. SUGGESTIVE-TO-SOLID (policy think tank).

The Partition story is real for weaving generally (weavers from Sindh, Jhang, Multan arrived 1947),  
but the carpet-specific migration runs east-to-west.

AND PANIPAT IS THE WORLD'S LARGEST TEXTILE-RECYCLING CLUSTER, only \~25 years old:  
30 lakh kg of recycled yarn per day, \~200 spinning mills, \~70,000 workers. (The Tribune, 2025\)  
₹7,000 crore of carpets exported annually. (Amar Ujala, 2023\)  
The cheap tufted rug and the recycled-fibre industry are the same story.

── THE TOOLS ──

TWO INDEPENDENT NON-COMMERCIAL SOURCES SAY THE COMMON INDIAN TOOL IS UNPOWERED:  
CUTS International: "a tufting machine, which resembles a pair of large scissors, is used for  
weaving the carpets. THE TUFTING MACHINE COSTS AROUND INR 100." (\~US$1.20)  
Harvard FXB / Kara: "a screwdriver-shaped, HAND-OPERATED 'gun'"  
SOLID that a large share of Indian hand-tufting is done with an unpowered hand punch.

IICT BHADOHI'S OFFICIAL SYLLABUS TEACHES BOTH AS SEPARATE SKILLS:  
"Practice of tufting using HAND & ELECTRIC GUN" (ECT-551 practical)  
"tufting equipments used e.g. (tufting frame, tufting gun etc.) Schortz hand tufted machine"  
— Government of India institute syllabus. SOLID.  
The manual tool is taught as first-class technique, not a legacy curiosity.

INDIA NOW EXPORTS TUFTING GUNS: Sheru Brothers Engg. Works, Meerut, est. 1965 — electric hand  
tufting guns (\~₹9,000, 300W, 1425 rpm), exporting to EGYPT AND SOUTH AFRICA. SOLID.

INDIA PATENTS THE BACKING, NOT THE GUN: IICT filed a patent for a new tufted-carpet backing  
technique; K.K. Goswami invented a carpet backing technology called "Snehabha." No Indian tufting-  
gun patent found. India's documented technical contribution is on the latex/backing side. SOLID.

── VOCABULARY ──

गुच्छित कालीन (guchchhit kālīn) — from गुच्छ, "tuft/cluster" — IS the Hindi word for tufted carpet.  
It appears on Hindi Wikipedia and essentially NOWHERE ELSE. The trade uses English transliteration:  
हैंड टफ्टेड (hand tufted), हैंडनाॅटेड (hand-knotted), टफ्टिंग गन (tufting gun) — English loanwords in  
Devanagari, even in a Hindi-medium paper writing for a Hindi-speaking carpet town.

Compare the old crafts, which are lexically rich: दरी (durrie), गलीचा (ghalicha), तालीम (talim, the  
coded weaving notation), रफ़ूगर (rafoogar, darner/restorer), बुनकर (weaver).

The absence of indigenous tufting vocabulary is itself evidence of a late, foreign, ready-made  
technology transfer.

Etymology: "'कालीन' अरबी शब्द है जबकि 'ग़लीचा' फ़ारसी शब्द" — "'Kālīn' is Arabic while 'ghalīcha' is  
Persian" — from which one infers the art came to India with the Mughals.

── THE 1980s DUST-MITE PANIC — NO ENGLISH EQUIVALENT ──

"1980年あたりから不当なダニ問題が湧き上がり"  
"From around 1980 an UNFAIR DUST-MITE PROBLEM arose."  
Japanese carpet lost to hard flooring because of a health scare about mites. This is the single  
biggest fact in modern Japanese carpet history and it has no counterpart in the US/UK record.  
Production data confirms the long decline: tufted output 51,345 → 35,779 thousand m² (2009→2024).  
Tufted is \~99% of Japanese output by area; woven is a rounding error. SOLID.

── THE DANTSU TRADITION, AND A LOVELY REVERSAL ──

SAKAI: "天保2年（1831年）堺の糸物商藤本庄左衛門が泉利兵衛に織らせて堺緞通として売り出したのが始め"  
"It began in Tenpō 2 (1831) when the Sakai yarn merchant Fujimoto Shōzaemon had Izumi Rihei weave  
it and put it on sale as Sakai dantsu." — City of Sakai. SOLID.  
His grandson 藤本荘太郎 was called 緞通王, "the dantsu king," and 「渡米して海外への普及に努めた」 —  
HE TRAVELLED TO AMERICA TO SPREAD IT ABROAD.  
→ A Japanese carpet man was selling into America in the Meiji era, SEVENTY YEARS BEFORE the tufting  
machine came the other way.

NABESHIMA: origin in the Genroku era (1688–1704). The third Saga lord 鍋島綱茂 granted a rice stipend  
to protect the technique, made it an official domain product, and BANNED ITS SALE TO THE GENERAL  
PUBLIC. SOLID.

The German flooring trade journal "boden wand decke" (founded 1954\) quoting ITS OWN SEPTEMBER 1959

"Offensichtlich befindet sich das Tuftingverfahren, das in Deutschland erst seit kurzer Zeit und

It names the three German tufting producers as of 1959:
