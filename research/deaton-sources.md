# Deaton, *Bedspreads to Broadloom*: bibliography and source trail

Thomas M. Deaton, *Bedspreads to Broadloom: The Story of the Tufted Carpet Industry* (Acton, Mass.: Tapestry Press, 1993). The bibliography (pp. 209–217) was transcribed from photographs of Tim Eads's copy, Oct 2026. The book has no notes, so the bibliography is the only trail from its claims to their sources.

Spelling follows the book, including its slips: "Capitol" for Capital, "Ellijah" for Ellijay, "Eton" (probably Eton, Ga.), and "Whatley" next to "Whaley" for what may be the same author. Entries are flagged **primary** where they are first-hand: documents, interviews, or articles written at the time or by participants.

"Where" is filled in as each source is traced. It records where the source can be read now, or that it is still to find.

## What to chase first

1. **The interview transcripts.** Confirm that the Bandy Heritage Center's Thomas Deaton collection (2010.003) holds them. Ask for Moench, Looper's daughter, Albert Cobble, Paul Jolley, Lewis Card, Mose Painter, W. R. and Addie Evans, Hugh Barrow (DuPont) and R. E. Hamilton (TTMA). This is a visit or an email to the Center, and is also in the Claude in Chrome brief.
2. **Catherine Evans Whitener's own account,** *The Dalton News*, 11 March 1937. Dalton newspapers on microfilm (the Dalton-Whitfield public library and Crown Gardens & Archives, Dalton, both to confirm).
3. **Windham, *The Tufting Machine* (3rd ed., 1941).** A machine-fixers' manual from the decisive decade.
4. **Ewing and Norton (1955)** for Bigelow and broadloom, and **Reynolds (1968)** for 1947–63.
5. **R. E. Hamilton's 1941 articles** (*Christian Science Monitor*, *Reader's Digest*): contemporary national coverage.
6. **The Sims Textile map (c. 1946)** at the historical society; it fits the open question on Sims Textile.
